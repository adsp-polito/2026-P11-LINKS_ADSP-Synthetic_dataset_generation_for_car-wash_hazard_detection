# results

Evaluation outputs that show whether the pipeline meets its targets.

| Report | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Pass rate per class | At least 50% of each class's images pass validation |
| Agreement with human review | At least 80% on a sample |
| Mask quality | At least 90% of masks reach IoU 0.5 against a hand-labelled sample |

Commit the reports and their configuration. Generated images stay out of git.
